Terroir at a Glance

Left Bank

Médoc & Graves

Gravelly, fast-draining soil that favors Cabernet Sauvignon: firm tannin, cassis and graphite fruit, cedar and tobacco-leaf aromatics, built to age for decades.

Right Bank

Saint-Émilion & Pomerol

Cooler clay and limestone that suits Merlot and Cabernet Franc: plusher, more perfumed in youth — red plum, black cherry, mocha, violets.

Whites & Sauternes

Graves & Sauternais

Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon make age-worthy dry whites, while noble rot along the Garonne yields the honeyed, apricot-scented dessert wines of Sauternes.
